# Chapter Outline Template
Scene-level chapter planning structure for AI-assisted drafting.

---

## usage_notes

- One file per chapter or per act block
- Fill every field before handing to a drafting AI
- `authorial_intent_and_subtext` is a note **for the AI only** — it should be conveyed through action, dialogue, and description, never stated directly in prose
- `chapter_summary_for_writer` is generated after scenes are complete — it's the connective tissue that becomes the drafting brief

---

## chapter_[number]

**instructions:** [Write Chapter [NUMBER]. The POV is strictly [CHARACTER] in [POV/TENSE]. Follow the outline's chapter summary and emotional shift precisely.]
**tentpole_tag:** [If applicable — e.g., Inciting Incident, Midpoint Reversal, Dark Night of the Soul, Climax]
**pov_details:** [POV Character Name], [Point of View and Tense — e.g., Third Person Limited, Past Tense]
**chapter_goal:** [What is the single most important thing this chapter needs to accomplish for the plot or a character arc?]
**authorial_intent_and_subtext:** [Hidden note for the AI. Describe the underlying goal of the chapter, any dramatic irony at play, or subtext that must be conveyed through action, dialogue, and description rather than narration. The POV character and reader should feel this — not be told it.]
**emotional_shift:** [The overall emotional arc for the POV character from beginning to end. e.g., "From curious and hopeful to betrayed and devastated."]
**end_of_chapter_hook:** [The final cliffhanger, reveal, or unresolved question that makes the reader turn the page.]
