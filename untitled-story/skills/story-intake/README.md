# story-intake — credit and provenance

## Credit

This skill is adapted from **"30 to Glory"** by **Ekello Harrid** (GitHub
[FuFicFac](https://github.com/FuFicFac), [portfolio](https://ej-student-portal.vercel.app/)),
published as a gist:
<https://gist.github.com/FuFicFac/f29ecac587c175280d863bb7ef766222>

His design carries the substance of the skill: the 22/4/4 question budget and
the hard stop at 30, the six worksheet-critical coverage zones, the
`COVERED`/`PARTIAL`/`THIN`/`UNRESOLVED` markers, the reserve-question logic, the
`READY`/`NOT_READY` gate, and the four act structures with the
ten-chapters-per-act rule. The three documents under `references/` are his,
reproduced unmodified.

What is local to this project: retargeting the handoff away from
`book-brain-builder` and into `kb/` + `work/outline/`, the seeding table, the
escape hatch, and the `AGENTS.md` / muse wiring.

## The reference files

`SKILL.md` reads three documents from `references/`, all tracked in git:

- `references/story-worksheet.md` — full worksheet template, all field definitions
- `references/act-structures.md` — the 3/4/5/9-act guide and decision framework
- `references/chapter-outline.md` — chapter outline template

They are reproduced unmodified from the gist, which keeps them easy to diff
against the source if it changes. Local adaptations belong in `SKILL.md` or this
file, not in the reference documents.

`act-structures.md` is itself a synthesis that cites Reedsy, MasterClass,
Writer's Edit, Dabble, and The Novel Smithy. Worth knowing if that material is
ever extended or rewritten.
